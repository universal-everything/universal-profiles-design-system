# 0013 The solid closed UP! container cube replaces the flat-badge depiction in the twelve slide backgrounds

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-09-16
Closes: none (OPEN-03 and OPEN-04 stay open; this record corrects the geometry of an admitted depiction and the composition language around it, it admits no mark file and makes no trademark statement)

## Context

Decision 0012 replaced the twelve generic slide backgrounds under `../assets/generated/backgrounds/slides-v2/` with scenes that depict the official UP! box, generated from the reference the owner had pointed to at the time: Universal Profile Board node 1131:25142 (Box 5a), a front-facing render of the rounded badge face, since superseded. Generated from it, every UP! object in the twelve files read as a flat rounded badge or tile: a rounded square with the mark, without depth.

A first correction was staged on 2026-09-15 and never published: twelve precise-object edits of the flat-badge files guided by the create-profile raw image on the same board (frame 1737:1009, node 1737:1014, a private export cited by hash; since superseded). The generator took that open-receptacle illustration literally, and the cubes gained top apertures, lids, document-like inserts and malformed protrusions. The product owner rejected that draft (authenticated private conversation, cited by date, not reproduced): the Universal Profile is a container, but the object that stands for it is a solid, closed, glossy cube. What the profile holds is shown by the ecosystem around the cube, never by opening the cube. The owner also pointed at the richer original branding language of the shipped onboarding collages, above all `get-started-2.png`: one dominant UP! cube anchoring a dense, asymmetrical, layered composition of translucent identity cards, avatar shapes, lock, envelope, photo, music, fashion and cultural objects, pearlescent ribbons and soft geometry, with overlap, scale contrast and foreground, middle-ground and background depth.

## Decision

1. The geometry contract. In every Universal Profiles presentation image the UP! object is a solid, closed and sealed container cube: a monolithic rounded die with softly rounded edges; smooth, continuous top and side planes with no features; the exact white UP! mark once on the front face; no other readable text. There is no hole, slot, lid, rim, cavity or insert: no aperture, recessed shape, receptacle, handle or tab, and no document, card or object enters or emerges from a cube. It is never a flat badge, a rounded rectangular tile, a thin slab or a front-on app-icon shape. The reason is the product itself: the Universal Profile is a container, an account that holds things; the cube stands for it as one whole object, and the layered scene around the cube shows what it holds. `../brand/lockups.md` states the contract as the description of the cube; `../imagery/briefs.md` (IB-12) requires it of every prompt in the positive and requires every prompt to reject the openings in its Avoid line.
2. The composition language. The twelve backgrounds follow the shipped onboarding collages rather than isolated cubes in empty space: a layered ecosystem of frosted profile cards, faceless avatar medallions, lock, chat, music, camera and image tiles, headphones, sneakers, medals, spheres, ribbons and fine arcs around one or more UP! cubes, with deliberate overlap, varied scale, restrained iridescence and a measured text-safe zone left quiet for copy.
3. The twelve files are replaced. Each new file is a fresh generation from its prompt with two or three of the repository's already-public onboarding illustrations supplied as style and composition references (`get-started-2.png`, SHA-256 `3bfb7b9e4677c0caf1567d93d7cf47f468266b4a63648bf8de904dd33434d911`; `get-started-3.png`, SHA-256 `d12fc0ef848cce3453caeed4ced50ece1ace4b952f9d381c3b94b2dfd3af56f3`; `get-started-4.png`, SHA-256 `27ce8393a4d68044de59c520f5a7208191d57200176492df0187ec1b6d0d817a`; the shipped product art republished under decision 0011, recorded in `../assets/slides/onboarding/PROVENANCE.json`). No earlier published background and no private design-file export was an input. The selected files are `identity-orbits-light.png`, `identity-orbits-dark.png`, `glass-profile-stack-light.png`, `glass-profile-stack-dark.png`, `address-ribbons-light.png`, `address-ribbons-dark.png`, `modular-constellation-light.png`, `modular-constellation-dark.png`, `identity-network-light.png`, `identity-network-dark.png`, `iridescent-horizon-light.png` and `iridescent-horizon-dark.png`, all opaque 1672 by 941 files; the twelve generations were countersigned between 2026-09-16T06:43:40Z and 2026-09-16T06:58:43Z, and four of them were replaced later the same morning as set out below.
