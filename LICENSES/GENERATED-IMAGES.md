# Generated imagery

The twenty-eight images under `assets/generated/` (the hero pair, the title pair, the twelve dense slide backgrounds in `backgrounds/slides-v2/` and the twelve additive ambient slide backgrounds in `backgrounds/slides-v3-ambient/`) were produced for this repository with OpenAI's image-generation product (model `gpt-image 2.0`, as named by the content credentials embedded in the files). The verbatim prompts and, for the four edited dense files, the verbatim edit prompts including the superseded first edits of two chains; generation dates and ids; operator role; licence; SHA-256 hashes; dimensions and colour facts; references; cube counts; and measured text-safe zones are recorded in `assets/generated/PROVENANCE.json`, which the validator verifies. The staged prompt files in both background directories are kept byte-identical to their entries. The dense selection remains pinned in `scripts/validate/branded-backgrounds.json`; the ambient selection is independently pinned in `scripts/validate/ambient-backgrounds.json`.

- Each PNG carries a C2PA content-credentials manifest signed by OpenAI (software agent `gpt-image 2.0`, claim generator OpenAI Media Service API, digital source type "trained algorithmic media") and an invisible watermark declared in that manifest. The manifests are left intact; do not strip them, and expect tools that read content credentials to identify the images as AI-generated.
- They are original outputs created for this project in the system's own materials and colours. The hero pair and the title pair show blank objects only, with neither words nor marks and no people. The twelve dense slide backgrounds show the UP! mark on one or more official UP! container cubes, solid, closed and sealed, rendered by the generator inside layered scenes at the owner's request (`../decisions/0012-branded-slide-backgrounds.md`, corrected by `../decisions/0013-container-cube-slide-backgrounds.md`), and no other words, letters, marks or people; every other scene object is blank. Four of them are precise-object edits of their own generation output made on 2026-09-16: `backgrounds/slides-v2/glass-profile-stack-light.png` and `backgrounds/slides-v2/address-ribbons-dark.png` were first edited to remove a swoosh-like side stripe from the sneaker in the scene and then edited again to remove the sneaker altogether, replacing it (and, in the dark address-ribbons file, a blank periwinkle cube) with brand-neutral spheres and a plain medal; `backgrounds/slides-v2/identity-orbits-dark.png` had the top planes of its cubes re-rendered as smooth convex surfaces; `backgrounds/slides-v2/identity-network-light.png` lost a sculpture behind its central cube. The edit prompts, the edit ids, the superseded outputs' hashes and the visual review of each result are on their provenance entries; no footwear remains in the two formerly affected files.
- The twelve ambient slide backgrounds are a separate proposed family under decision 0014. They show twenty official closed UP! container cubes in total, one or two per scene, with large quiet areas for copy-heavy slides; no people, faces, footwear, rockets, third-party marks or uncontrolled text appear. Light files take ink copy and dark files take white copy inside the recorded safe zone. Their exact PNG bytes and C2PA manifests are retained. Their references are public repository files or earlier ambient outputs except for one unpublished superseded mist-orbit-right dark draft retained by hash only in the dark mist-orbit lineage; it has no public path and is not published or licensed by decision 0014.
- They are delivered at the generator's native size (1672 by 941), 8-bit RGB without alpha; the larger formats listed in the briefs have not been produced. Every file is checked against the slide contract in the provenance record (`node scripts/validate.mjs --only rasters`).
- The four generated-background overview sheets under `assets/slides/previews/` are deterministic derived compositions and inherit these terms for their background pixels. The app screens composed into the two mixed app-showcase previews are covered by `assets/screenshots/mobile-app/PROVENANCE.json`, not by this file.
- They are licensed with the repository under the Apache License 2.0 to the extent that copyright subsists in them. Because the copyright status of machine-generated images varies by jurisdiction, no warranty of exclusivity is given; use remains subject to the terms of the generation product used.
- Do not present them as photographs of real products or as official marks. Do not edit them into marks.
- New generated images follow the same rule: the appropriate brief from `imagery/briefs.md` (dense backgrounds: IB-12; ambient backgrounds: IB-13), a provenance entry with the prompt, hash, register, references, visual-review facts where required and measured safe zone, and no private board render as a generation input. The twelve dense slide backgrounds were generated with the repository's already-public onboarding illustrations (`assets/slides/onboarding/get-started-2.png`, `get-started-3.png`, `get-started-4.png`, republished under decision 0011) as style and composition references, recorded by hash on every entry; the private board exports behind the earlier, superseded depictions (node 1131:25142 and node 1737:1014, superseded) were cited by file key, node id and hash only and are not published. The ambient lineage follows the boundary in the preceding bullet. A further branded image needs its own recorded authorization.
- The shipped onboarding illustrations under `assets/slides/onboarding/` and the app screens under `assets/screenshots/mobile-app/` are not generated images and are not covered here; see `THIRD-PARTY.md` and their provenance records.

## Embedded marks in the twenty-four slide backgrounds

The Apache-2.0 grant above covers the generated scene pixels of the slide backgrounds: the orbits, cards, ribbons, cubes, spheres, nodes, waves and mist. It does not cover the mark those scenes depict: the UP! mark remains a trademark of its owner and is not licensed for extraction or standalone reuse (see `../TRADEMARKS.md`).

- **Permitted:** using a whole file, unmodified apart from scaling and a crop that keeps the scene around the cubes, as a presentation background, a section or closing slide, a documentation image or a website background, inside the safe-zone and composition rules of `assets/generated/backgrounds/README.md`.
- **Not permitted:** cropping a cube out, tracing, vectorising, isolating or recolouring a cube or the mark; using a cube as a logo, icon, sticker, lockup, app icon or watermark; altering the mark; implying endorsement; presenting a rendered cube as an official mark file. Anyone who needs the mark as a mark obtains the official files from the owner (`assets/logos/README.md`).
- **Contact sheets:** the four background overviews under `assets/slides/previews/` show the same scenes at thumbnail size and inherit the same restriction: their thumbnails may not be used to extract the cubes or the mark.
- **Scope:** decisions 0012 and 0013 authorize publication and use of exactly the twelve pinned dense files and their two overview sheets; decision 0014 separately authorizes exactly the twelve pinned ambient files and their two overview sheets. These decisions are not brand approval and not licences to render the mark anew. Decision 0010 (no reconstruction of a mark under any label) stays in force for everything else.
