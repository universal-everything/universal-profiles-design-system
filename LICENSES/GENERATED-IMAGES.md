# Generated imagery

The sixteen images under `assets/generated/` (the hero pair, the title pair and the twelve slide backgrounds in `backgrounds/slides-v2/`) were produced for this repository with OpenAI's image-generation product (model `gpt-image 2.0`, as named by the content credentials embedded in the files); the verbatim prompts, the generation dates, the operator role, the licence, the SHA-256 hash, dimensions and colour facts of each file and its measured text-safe zone are recorded in `assets/generated/PROVENANCE.json`, which the validator verifies. The staged prompt file `assets/generated/backgrounds/slides-v2/PROMPTS.source.json` is kept byte-identical to the entries, and the twelve slide backgrounds are additionally pinned in `scripts/validate/branded-backgrounds.json`.

- Each PNG carries a C2PA content-credentials manifest signed by OpenAI (software agent `gpt-image 2.0`, claim generator OpenAI Media Service API, digital source type "trained algorithmic media") and an invisible watermark declared in that manifest. The manifests are left intact; do not strip them, and expect tools that read content credentials to identify the images as AI-generated.
- They are original outputs created for this project in the system's own materials and colours. The hero pair and the title pair show blank objects only, with neither words nor marks and no people. The twelve slide backgrounds show the UP! mark on one or more official 3D UP! boxes, rendered by the generator from the owner's reference at the owner's request (`../decisions/0012-branded-slide-backgrounds.md`), and no other words, letters, marks or people.
- They are delivered at the generator's native size (1672 by 941; two files 1671 by 941), 8-bit RGB without alpha; the larger formats listed in the briefs have not been produced. Every file is checked against the slide contract in the provenance record (`node scripts/validate.mjs --only rasters`).
- The previews under `assets/slides/previews/` that show them are derived compositions and inherit these terms for the background pixels; the app screens composed into two of them are covered by `assets/screenshots/mobile-app/PROVENANCE.json`, not by this file.
- They are licensed with the repository under the Apache License 2.0 to the extent that copyright subsists in them. Because the copyright status of machine-generated images varies by jurisdiction, no warranty of exclusivity is given; use remains subject to the terms of the generation product used.
- Do not present them as photographs of real products or as official marks. Do not edit them into marks.
- New generated images follow the same rule: a brief from `imagery/briefs.md` (backgrounds: IB-12), a provenance entry with the prompt, hash, register and safe zone, and no reuse of private board renders as inputs. The one recorded exception is the official UP! box reference behind the twelve slide backgrounds, used under the owner's authorization and cited by node id and hash; the standalone reference is not published, and a further branded image needs its own recorded authorization.
- The shipped onboarding illustrations under `assets/slides/onboarding/` and the app screens under `assets/screenshots/mobile-app/` are not generated images and are not covered here; see `THIRD-PARTY.md` and their provenance records.

## Embedded marks in the twelve slide backgrounds

The Apache-2.0 grant above covers the generated scene pixels of the slide backgrounds: the orbits, cards, ribbons, cubes, spheres, nodes, waves and mist. It does not cover the mark those scenes depict: the UP! mark remains a trademark of its owner and is not licensed for extraction or standalone reuse (see `../TRADEMARKS.md`).

- **Permitted:** using a whole file, unmodified apart from scaling and a crop that keeps the scene around the boxes, as a presentation background, a section or closing slide, a documentation image or a website background, inside the safe-zone and composition rules of `assets/generated/backgrounds/README.md`.
- **Not permitted:** cropping a box out, tracing, vectorising, isolating or recolouring a box or the mark; using a box as a logo, icon, sticker, lockup, app icon or watermark; altering the mark; implying endorsement; presenting a rendered box as an official mark file. Anyone who needs the mark as a mark obtains the official files from the owner (`assets/logos/README.md`).
- **Contact sheets:** the two background overviews under `assets/slides/previews/` show the same scenes at thumbnail size and inherit the same restriction: their thumbnails may not be used to extract the boxes or the mark.
- **Scope:** the authorization behind these files is a publication and use decision for the twelve pinned files, recorded in `../decisions/0012-branded-slide-backgrounds.md`; it is not brand approval and not a licence to render the mark anew. Decision 0010 (no reconstruction of a mark under any label) stays in force for everything else.
