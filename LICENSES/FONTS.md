# Fonts

The system specifies two typefaces and bundles neither.

| Face | Licence | Where to obtain | Use here |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inter | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | The Inter project's official distribution; also bundled by the LUKSO base-layer package | Interface and marketing text |
| PT Mono | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | ParaType's official distribution; also bundled by the base-layer package | Names, suffixes, addresses, balances |

Under the OFL the fonts may be used, embedded and redistributed with their licence files, may not be sold on their own, and may not be renamed if modified. Products that bundle the fonts include the licence text with the font files; this repository references the faces by name only. Verify the licence text shipped with the specific font files in use.
