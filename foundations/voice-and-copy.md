# Voice and copy

Status: observed for product strings and sanctioned lines; normalized for the terminology table; proposed for the voice rule (no brand voice was ever formally signed off, OPEN-15)

## Voice rule

Clear, direct, human, confident. Second person. Plain words for hard concepts. One metaphor at a time (passport, face, reputation). Sentence case everywhere except the wordmark lockup and uppercase label roles. Never hype, never price talk, never counts. Reserve stage aphorisms for hero moments and standards numbers for builder documentation.

Registers, from most to least authoritative:

1. **Product voice** (shipped strings): short, warm, reassuring about control and safety, honest about consequences. Playful exclamations belong to onboarding only; errors are calm and specific and never end in an exclamation mark.
2. **Stage voice** (2026 public talk): declarative, contrarian, one accent word per line. Used for headlines, not for body copy.
3. **Brand voice** (2024 foundations): aspirational and unifying; slightly dated. Use its positioning lines, not its adjectives.

Not brand voice: creator-script hype, all-caps emphasis, market comparisons, price or count claims.

## Sanctioned lines

Consumer: "Your Profile for the New Web3." · "Your Universal Profile is the smart profile for the next era." · "One profile. Every chain. Every app." · "A new type of blockchain account that works like a profile, not a wallet." · "Create your forever profile." · "Choose your forever address." · "Truly only yours."

Stage: "An account, not an address." · "The missing piece was identity." · "You can make a hundred wallets. You cannot make a hundred reputations." · "Everything you cannot buy." · "Culture needs infrastructure." · "Presence is valuable, and it should be earned, not bought."

Builder: "The first fully standardized smart contract account." · "LSPs make up Universal Profiles." · "Infrastructure for Culture."

Parent-brand lines (only with LUKSO branding): "The blockchain built for social, culture and creators." · "Powered by LUKSO."

## Content rules for surfaces

- Titles are nouns or short verb phrases; buttons are verbs ("Send", "Follow", "Let's go!" only in onboarding).
- Consequence first in destructive copy: what will happen, then what to do. Irreversible actions use slide-to-confirm and say so.
- Errors: what happened, why if known, what to do next. No blame, no jargon, no exclamation marks.
- Empty states: one line that names the thing that is empty and one action; no apologies.
- Network context: name the network in words next to its dot; "Testnet" is always written, never implied by colour.
- Never hard-code counts (profiles, validators, users), prices or market figures.
- Named teams over an anonymous "we" in marketing.

## Do not say

"Wallet" for the product, "closed beta", "waitlist", "Discover", "smart wallet", price or market-cap comparisons, "mascot", any dated metric.
