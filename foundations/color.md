# Colour

Status: observed for the neutral, status and network families; normalized for the AA-safe steps and roles; proposed for the UP accent family and the dark theme (OPEN-01, OPEN-07)

Colour in Universal Profiles has three jobs, in this order: keep the canvas quiet, let the profile's own address-derived colour show, and signal state. The accent is a frame, never the subject.

## Palette structure

| Tier | Groups | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Primitive | `color.neutral`, `color.up`, `color.green`, `color.red`, `color.yellow`, `color.blue`, `color.purple`, `color.lukso`, `color.sky`, `color.honey`, `color.network`, `color.static`, `color.legacy` | Never used directly in components except through a semantic or component token. Base-layer families are the exact output of the shared HSL generator and are not redefined here. |
| Semantic | `surface.*`, `text.*`, `border.*`, `accent.*`, `interactive.*`, `status.*`, `network.*`, `avatar.*`, `gradient.*`, `glass.*` | The names components use. Each resolves per theme. |
| Component | `button.*`, `input.*`, `tag.*`, `profile-card.*`, and so on | Reference semantic or primitive tokens only. |

## Neutral base (observed)

Hue 206, saturation 30 percent, steps 10 to 100. `color.neutral.98` is the canvas, `color.neutral.20` the ink, `color.neutral.90` the default border and fallback fill, `color.neutral.35` the focus border and the hue of every shadow. These values are shared with the base layer and consumed by both products.

## The UP accent (proposed, anchor open)

`color.up` is hue 229, saturation 78 percent, generated by the same HSL generator as the base families. The ramp is anchored on the labelled periwinkle swatch of the brand board, so `color.up.67` equals `#6981EC` exactly, and it passes close to the board's `#8494EE` (`color.up.73`) and to the shipped app icon gradient `#A7ACF9` to `#6E7BE6` (`color.up.82` to `color.up.67`).

What the evidence proves and does not prove:

- The brand board shows three periwinkle values and white (`color.up.observed.board-1`, `board-2`, `board-3`) without roles, variable bindings or approval. They are recorded as observed values, not as rules.
- No shipped product surface consumes an accent today. The mobile app uses `color.purple.51` for four secondary labels; the web relies on neutrals.
- Every UP artefact since 2024 sits in the 227 to 247 degree band; the app icon is the only shipped asset with the colour.

Until the anchor is confirmed (OPEN-01), components use the accent only through the roles below and never call the board values primary or secondary.

| Role | Light | Dark | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `accent.default`, `text.link` | `color.up.56` | `color.up.78` | Links, accent text and icons on canvas, card and the opaque glass tint |
| `accent.fill`, `accent.on-fill` | `color.up.56` with white | `color.up.73` with `color.neutral.10` | Accent buttons and selected controls; the dark fill inverts like the primary button |
| `accent.fill-pressed` | `color.up.50` | `color.up.78` | Pressed accent fill |
| `accent.brand` | `color.up.67` | `color.up.73` | Large type, icons, fills that carry no text; the lockup tint on light surfaces |
| `accent.soft`, `accent.soft-border` | `color.up.96`, `color.up.86` | `color.up.22`, `color.up.38` | Selected rows and accent chips |
| `accent.gradient-start`, `accent.gradient-end` | `color.up.82` to `color.up.67` | same | The brand gradient, top to bottom or left to right, mirroring the app icon |

Accent text never sits on translucent glass: the composite over a dark cover drops below 3:1 (contrast row L-56). Use `text.default` or the solid glass tint there.

## Status colours (normalized)

Observed production steps are kept for icons and fills; text and control steps move to darker members of the same scales so every pair passes AA.

| State | Text | Fill and label | Control | Soft tint |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Success | `status.success.text` (green 30) | `status.success.fill` with white | `status.success.control` (green 35, the switch on-state) | `status.success.soft` |
| Error | `status.error.text` (red 45) | `status.error.fill` with white | `status.error.border` (red 55, observed input border) | `status.error.soft` |
| Warning and testnet | `status.warning.text` (yellow 25) | `status.warning.fill` (yellow 65) with `text.default` | `status.warning.icon` (yellow 35) | `status.warning.soft` |
| Info (proposed) | `status.info.text` (blue 40) | `status.info.fill` with white | | `status.info.soft` |

The observed success check `color.green.54` stays as `status.success.icon` for decorative use and dark surfaces; it reaches only 2.06:1 on white.

## Network colours (observed, one placeholder)

`network.lukso-mainnet` is LUKSO magenta `#FE005B` and is reserved for the LUKSO network and the LYX token: token logos, network dots, the LYX disc. `network.lukso-testnet` is the yellow 65 step (production hard-codes a value one unit away; the palette value wins). `network.base` is Base blue. `network.ethereum` is a placeholder until the product fixes a value (OPEN-05). Network colour is shown as a dot or logo next to ink text; it is never the text colour on a light surface and never the only signal.

## Surfaces and text roles

See [elevation-and-glass.md](elevation-and-glass.md) for the tier model. Text roles: `text.default` (ink, 12.63:1 on white), `text.muted` (neutral 40, secondary text and the suffix, 5.72:1), `text.placeholder` (neutral 45), `text.inverse`, `text.link`, `text.on-glass-label`. `text.subtle` (neutral 60) exists only for migration and may never carry information on its own.

## Do and do not

- Do derive profile colour from the address with `packages/address-signature`; never hand-pick a profile colour.
- Do keep magenta for LUKSO network and LYX roles only.
- Do not add colours outside `tokens/src`. Off-palette literals found in production (`#1C2A3A`, `#E5E5E5`, the platform system green in the glass toggle, utility-framework greys in developer screens) are replaced by tokens, see `../provenance/reconciliation.md`.
- Do not use the observed board values or the legacy families in new work.
