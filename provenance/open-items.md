# Open items register

Every decision this design system could not close from evidence. Tokens carry the matching identifier in `$extensions.up.open`; documents cite it inline. An item closes only when the owner named in the "Closes when" column records the decision in `decisions/` and the affected tokens or documents are updated in the same change.

Status legend: **open** (no decision yet), **partially closed** (evidence narrowed the options), **closed** (recorded in `decisions/`).
