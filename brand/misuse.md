# Misuse

Status: normalized (rules derived from observed marks and the retired directions)

## Marks

- Do not create, modify, stylise, rotate, outline-and-fill, or recolour the UP!, LUKSO or Universal Everything marks. Use official files only.
- Do not invent a mark. AI-generated logos, invented restylings of the badge or wordmark, and any reconstruction of the missing masters are prohibited (`../decisions/0010-no-reconstruction-under-any-label.md`).
- Do not crop, trace or extract the UP! container cubes out of any of the twenty-four branded slide backgrounds or their overview sheets, and do not use them as a logo, icon, sticker or lockup. The expressive authorization is decision 0012 and decision 0013 for exactly twelve scenes and their two overview sheets; decision 0014 covers exactly the twelve ambient scenes and their two sheets. The mark stays unlicensed and the decisions grant no extraction or trademark licence. Do not generate a new depiction of a mark without a new recorded authorization.
- Do not depict the UP! object as a flat badge, a rounded rectangular tile, a thin slab or a front-on app-icon shape in presentation imagery, and do not give it a hole, slot, lid, rim, cavity or insert or let anything enter or emerge from it. The Universal Profile is a container, but the object that stands for it is the solid, closed and sealed container cube: a monolithic rounded die with smooth, continuous top and side planes and the white UP! once on the front face; what the profile holds is shown by the scene around the cube (`../decisions/0013-container-cube-slide-backgrounds.md`).
- Do not render the badge in magenta or any colour outside the brand gradient, the accent, ink or white.
- Do not use "UP! CREATOR SPACE" or other sub-brand lockups until approved (OPEN-11).
- Do not place a lockup under event collateral or over busy covers without a glass or solid tint behind it.
- Do not use the LUKSO hexagon as a Universal Profiles mark, and do not name it as one in code (the mobile component that renders it is misnamed, see `../provenance/reconciliation.md`).

## Visual language

- No neon glow, glow outlines, circuit boards, hex grids, lens flares, cyberpunk palettes or laser effects.
- No generic glassmorphism outside the defined glass tier; no glass on the web until OPEN-08 is decided.
- No gradients other than the brand gradient and the address gradient.
- No magenta, pink dreamscape landscapes, dot-matrix arrows or pale-pink canvases on Universal Profiles surfaces; those belong to the parent brand and the foundation's reports. No generic AI dreamscapes either; the iridescent-horizon slides-v2 family (a periwinkle-white landscape in the system's materials, decision 0013) is the one admitted landscape.
- No mascots, no stock photography of people using phones, no faces in generated imagery.
- No hand-picked profile colours; the identicon is never tinted or replaced.

## Copy

- Never call the product a wallet; never drop the `#XXXX` suffix; never show a bare hex address when a name or identicon can be shown.
- Never quote prices, market figures or counts.
- Never reuse the board's pasted voice card or the cheat-sheet posters marked not for use.
