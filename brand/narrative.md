# Narrative

Status: observed for shipped and public lines; normalized for the pillar table; open for the board's mission and vision text and the pasted voice card (OPEN-15)

## What a Universal Profile is

Consumer: a smart profile, a new type of blockchain account that works like a profile, not a wallet, and a digital passport that represents a person, an organisation or a device. Newest framing (2026): an account that *is* the person, community or brand, carrying the name, the connections, the creations and the history of what it was given, readable by every app it visits. **An account, not an address.**

Technical: an LSP0 ERC725Account (execution, key-value storage, signature verification, universal receiver) owned by an LSP6 Key Manager that grants permissions to controllers, with LSP3 profile metadata, LSP5 and LSP12 asset registries, LSP26 followers and LSP7 and LSP8 tokens. Relayed gasless execution follows LSP15 and LSP25.

Who can have one: individuals, brands, communities, DAOs, apps, AI agents and devices.

## Four pillars

| Pillar | Plain meaning | Line in use |
|---|---|---|
| Cross-chain | The same profile address can be deployed on other EVM networks; today the app supports deployment to Base and Ethereum in addition to LUKSO | "One profile. Every chain. Every app." Say "can be deployed to other networks", never "works on every chain". |
| Gasless | A relayer pays gas so people never need the native token to start | "No gas on LUKSO." |
| Metadata | Unlimited on-chain profile data: name, avatar, bio, links, tags, plus standardized asset registries | "As unique as you are." |
| Permissions | Controllers with granular, revocable permissions across devices, apps and agents; recovery through controllers | "{controllerName} can update all your profile data." |

Supporting concepts: universal receiver (accounts react to incoming transactions), upgradeability, social recovery, safe transfers, followers, identicon (the visual checksum of the address), relayer, mini-apps, the Grid, Proof of Presence, Token Creator, cultural currencies.

## Commitments

- **Culture over finance.** Tokens are cultural tools: signals of participation, badges of belonging. No trading imagery, no price talk on Universal Profiles surfaces.
- **Self-sovereignty without fear.** Truly only yours; no one else controls the account, not even the makers; recoverable and revocable; no seed phrases.
- **Identity travels.** One profile across apps and chains, with LUKSO as the home chain and identity anchor.
- **Earned, not bought.** Proof of Presence: mintable supply, one item per profile, non-transferable, revocable.
- **Named people, not "we".** The ecosystem speaks through named teams and builders.
