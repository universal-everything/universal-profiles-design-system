# Relationship to LUKSO

Status: observed for the mark and colour split; normalized for the appearance rules

Universal Profiles is a product layer on the LUKSO base layer. The two share type, neutrals and the identicon, and differ in accent, marks and imagery.

| Aspect | LUKSO (parent brand and network) | Universal Profiles (product) |
|---|---|---|
| Mark | Rounded hexagon with a hexagonal aperture; wordmark with the open K | UP! badge, two-line wordmark, periwinkle cube |
| Colour | Magenta `#FE005B` for the mark, the LYX token and the mainnet signal; the foundation's pale pink and mint belong to its reports and decks | Periwinkle accent family `color.up`; neutrals shared |
| Imagery | Dot-matrix arrows, pink fabric and silk, pastel landscapes (reports and merch) | Soft-plastic periwinkle objects in frosted glass; address gradients |
| Voice | "The blockchain built for social, culture and creators." Builder register with standards numbers | "An account, not an address." Consumer register with the builder register in documentation |
| Type | Inter (marketing); SF faces appear only in ideation | Inter and PT Mono |

## When the parent brand appears on a Universal Profiles surface

- "Powered by LUKSO" in the product lockup on posters, decks and the app's about screens.
- The LUKSO mark as the LYX token logo and the LUKSO network logo, in magenta on light surfaces or black where a monochrome logo row is required.
- Network tags: "LUKSO Mainnet" with a magenta dot, "LUKSO Testnet" with the warning-yellow dot and the word Testnet always written.
- Never as the profile accent, never as the badge colour, never as a background.

## Universal Everything

The web home of profiles, with its own black E mark and the "UNIVERSAL / EVERYTHING" wordmark. It shares the two-weight wordmark construction with the Universal Profiles lockup; that family trait is preserved. Universal Everything marks are used only on its own surfaces and the app's home grid tile.
