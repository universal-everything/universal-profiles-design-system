# Image briefs

Status: proposed (production briefs; IB-01 is implemented procedurally, IB-02, IB-10, IB-12 and IB-13 have delivered assets under `../assets/generated/`)

Global rules for every brief: no neon rim light, no circuit-board or hex-grid textures, no lens flares, no invented logos or wordmarks, no readable interface text (blank bars instead), no faces or people, no magenta except where the LUKSO mark is explicitly requested, no generic AI dreamscape landscapes (the one admitted landscape is the product-specific iridescent-horizon family of IB-12, frosted acrylic hills, water and terraces in the system's own materials and colours, whose light prompt asks for a "Dreamlike but premium pale landscape" and is pinned as generated under `../decisions/0013-container-cube-slide-backgrounds.md`; it is not a licence for pink parent-brand dreamscapes or generic fantasy scenery). Every output ships with a provenance record (method, tool or model, verbatim prompt, seed if available, date, operator, post-processing, licence) and light and dark variants. Colour anchors: key `accent.brand` (`#6981EC`), highlight `color.up.82` (`#ADBAF5`), shadow `color.up.50` (`#1C41E3`); canvases `#F8FAFB` light and `#121B21` dark; ink `#243542`. The delivered IB-02 and IB-10 assets were produced with the earlier proposal anchors (`#7B83EA`, `#C2C5F5`, `#5862E4`), which sit in the same band; they remain valid.

Priority: IB-01, IB-02, IB-05, IB-03, IB-06, IB-04, IB-07, IB-08, IB-09, IB-10, IB-11, IB-12 and IB-13 (further variations require a new authorization when they depict the mark).

## IB-01 Address-gradient background set (procedural)

- **Why:** the Address Signature is the system's signature element; marketing needs a background family that belongs to Universal Profiles and requires no rights.
- **Method:** `packages/address-signature` from a real address: (a) the 90 degree linear gradient, (b) the aura: blob A at 22 percent by 30 percent with the first stop, blob B at 78 percent by 72 percent with the second, radius 55 percent of the short edge, alpha 30 percent (45 on dark), blur 12 percent of the short edge, 4 percent monochrome grain.
- **Formats:** SVG for both; raster exports at 2560 by 1440, 1440 by 2560, 1200 by 630, 1080 by 1080, 1080 by 1920; keep the darker stop away from the lower-right lockup area.
- **Status:** implemented; examples in `../assets/backgrounds/address-gradient/`. Presets for official profiles await OPEN-19.

## IB-12 Presentation background family (slides-v2)

- **Why:** the title pair (IB-10) is one quiet background; decks need a family of backgrounds with room for copy, in both registers, produced under recorded provenance. The product owner asked on 2026-09-15 for the first twelve to be visible in the repository and for many more variations (`../decisions/0011-owner-authorized-product-visuals.md`), and later that day for the generic set to be replaced by sophisticated scenes containing one or more actual UP! boxes (`../decisions/0012-branded-slide-backgrounds.md`); on 2026-09-16 the owner rejected a staged container-cube draft whose cubes showed apertures, lids and inserts and corrected the object to a solid, closed, sealed UP! cube inside the richer layered composition language of the shipped onboarding collages (`../decisions/0013-container-cube-slide-backgrounds.md`).
- **Shared prompt frame:** "Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: premium 16:9 presentation background, light or dark register, opaque." A layered Universal Profiles identity collage in the language of the shipped onboarding art: premium soft-plastic and frosted-acrylic 3D, pearlescent glass, restrained blush and cyan iridescence, deliberate overlap, varied scale, foreground, middle-ground and background depth, and a named quiet area for slide copy. Light register: white, cool mist, pale lavender and periwinkle. Dark register: deep ink blue, navy or indigo with periwinkle pools and edge light. Rules for a new prompt in this frame: no dominant pink, no neon, not cyberpunk (what each of the twelve delivered prompts actually says is summarised under Constraints below).
- **Families delivered (light and dark each), every file showing one or more official UP! container cubes:** identity orbits (a dense collage on the right two thirds around one large and three smaller cubes, left 28 percent by 75 percent text-safe); glass profile stack (a gallery wall of frosted profile cards on the left two thirds carrying five cubes, right 34 percent text-safe); address ribbons (layered pearlescent ribbons from the lower left to the upper right carrying five cubes in the light file and four in the dark, an upper centre-left band text-safe); modular constellation (object clusters framing the edges with two large corner-anchor cubes and three smaller ones, a centre column text-safe); identity network (a community cluster of three cubes, faceless avatar medallions and cultural objects on network lines on the right two thirds, left 30 percent text-safe); iridescent horizon (a low-horizon landscape of frosted hills, arches and terraces with three cubes at the lower right, upper-left half by 42 percent text-safe; the one landscape of the set, admitted under decision 0013 as a product-specific scene in the system's materials, not a generic dreamscape).
- **The container cube (the geometry every prompt must state):** the UP! object is a solid, closed and sealed container cube: a monolithic rounded die with softly rounded edges; smooth, continuous top and side planes with no features; the exact white "UP!" once on the front face; no other readable text; glossy periwinkle. The reason is the product: the Universal Profile is a container, but the cube that stands for it is one whole object, and the layered scene around it shows what it holds. There is no hole, slot, lid, rim, cavity or insert, and no document, card or object enters or emerges from a cube; it is never a flat badge, a rounded rectangular tile, a thin slab or a front-on app-icon shape. Every prompt states the closed geometry in the positive (solid and sealed, closed, monolithic, a smooth unbroken top, rounded, the white UP! on the front, no other readable text, opaque, a layered or dense scene) and carries an "Avoid" line that rejects holes, openings, slots, lids, rims and cavities, documents or objects entering or emerging from a cube, handles, tabs and protrusions, transparency and watermarks. The validator requires each of these points of every pinned prompt (`scripts/validate/branded-backgrounds.json`, `geometry`), whatever the prompt's hash, and rejects a prompt that states the closed geometry in the negative or that asks, in its affirmative text, for a listed opening, insert, object entering or emerging, or flat form (lidded, openable, slotted, hinged, hollow, sunken, concave or indented tops; cards, documents or objects that emerge from, spill out of, slide into or sit inside a cube); an Avoid clause, the negated part of a clause or a removal clause (an edit that removes an inset or a slot) that names a forbidden artifact never fails. That check is a keyword and pattern gate over the prompt text: wording outside its lists passes it, it cannot read the pixels, and it is not a proof of what a file shows; the geometry and the cube counts in the pixels rest on visual review, recorded as such in the provenance record.
- **Branded generation (the delivered set):** each file is a fresh generation from its prompt with two or three of the repository's already-public onboarding illustrations supplied as style and composition references (`../assets/slides/onboarding/get-started-2.png`, `get-started-3.png`, `get-started-4.png`; shipped product art republished under decision 0011, recorded by hash on every entry, not edited and not copied into any output). No earlier published background and no private design-file export was an input. Four of the twelve files were then replaced by precise-object edits of their own generation output: after the final review of 2026-09-16 found a swoosh-like side stripe on the sneaker in `glass-profile-stack-light.png` and `address-ribbons-dark.png`, a first edit removed the stripe and a second, at the owner's final visual cleanup the same day, removed the sneaker altogether and replaced it (and, in the dark address-ribbons file, a blank periwinkle cube) with brand-neutral spheres and a plain medal; the same cleanup re-rendered the cube tops of `identity-orbits-dark.png` as smooth continuous convex planes and removed the cone-shaped sculpture behind the central cube of `identity-network-light.png`. Every edit prompt is a precise-object edit that keeps the closed geometry and the closed or sealed cube tops as invariants, names the UP! cubes and preserves their lettering, edits only the named region, adds no new text, forbids a watermark and rejects openings and inserts on or from a cube in its Avoid line, with further rules per kind (object replacement, top-plane cleanup, occlusion removal); each edit's generation id, kind, verbatim prompt and superseded target by hash, and the first sneaker edits as superseded lineage, are recorded on the four entries, mirrored in `PROMPTS.source.json` and pinned with the edit-prompt contract in `scripts/validate/branded-backgrounds.json`. The earlier depictions came from private board exports that are now superseded for geometry: node `1131:25142` (Box 5a, a front-facing rounded badge render) produced the flat badges of the 0012 set, and the create-profile raw image node `1737:1014` in frame `1737:1009` produced the rejected open-receptacle draft of 2026-09-15, which was never published. Neither may be used to establish cube geometry again. This is owner-authorized for the twelve pinned files only; a further branded variation needs its own recorded authorization before it is generated for the repository.
- **Constraints (in substance in every prompt, in the prompts' own words rather than this sentence; the exact prompts are in `PROMPTS.source.json`):** the only permitted text or mark is the UP! mark on the official UP! container cubes (every prompt asks for the exact white "UP!" and "no other readable text"); every prompt asks for a layered or dense scene of frosted profile cards, faceless avatar medallions, cultural and lifestyle objects, spheres and ribbons rather than isolated cubes in empty space, and the two identity-network prompts require every human or profile representation to be a faceless icon and reject faces; the named text-safe area is asked for in all twelve (left 42 percent, right 40 percent, an upper-left or centre oval, a central ellipse of about 38 percent, left 38 percent, upper-left 44 percent) and the delivered zones were measured afresh because the dense scenes reach further in than asked; 16:9, fully opaque with transparency excluded in all twelve; a sparse or empty scene and a watermark are rejected in all twelve. Neon is named in four prompts only: the dark identity-orbits, glass-profile-stack and address-ribbons prompts say "not neon cyberpunk" and the dark iridescent-horizon prompt "not cyberpunk"; the other eight ask for restrained, gentle, controlled or subtle blush (or pink) and cyan iridescence or highlights and do not mention neon. Generic variations without an authorization keep the earlier frame instead: background only, all objects blank, nothing that reads as a word, letter or mark.
- **Formats:** the generator's native 16:9 (1672 by 941 delivered for all twelve); 1920 by 1080, 1080 by 1080 and 1080 by 1920 not yet produced. Every file must meet the slide contract in `../assets/generated/PROVENANCE.json` (aspect, minimum size, 8-bit RGB, intact content credentials, a declared and measured text-safe zone, a light and a dark file per family), which `node scripts/validate.mjs --only rasters` checks.
- **Status:** delivered (twelve files, the closed-cube set of 2026-09-16, which replaced the flat-badge set of 2026-09-15) under `../assets/generated/backgrounds/slides-v2/` with the verbatim prompts, generation ids and references in `PROMPTS.source.json` there and in the provenance record, pinned with the geometry contract in `scripts/validate/branded-backgrounds.json`; proposed, not approved brand imagery; the depicted cube is an observed mark under an owner-authorized publication and use decision (decisions 0012 and 0013), not brand approval, and the UP! mark stays its owner's trademark (no extraction or standalone reuse). New variations: copy a family prompt or write a new family inside the shared frame, generate both registers with the onboarding illustrations as references, record provenance, measure one safe zone that holds in both registers, recompose the contact sheets with `scripts/compose-previews.mjs`; see `../assets/generated/backgrounds/README.md`.

## IB-13 Ambient presentation background family (slides-v3-ambient)

- **Why:** IB-12 remains the expressive campaign, hero, section-divider and closing family. Content slides need quieter presentation infrastructure behind body copy, charts and transparent product screens without replacing or weakening IB-12. The owner requested this additive lower-contrast family on 2026-09-16; decision 0014 authorizes exactly its twelve scenes and two overview sheets.
- **Shared prompt frame:** “Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: subtle 16:9 presentation background, light or dark register, opaque.” The scene is ambient, subordinate, low contrast and edge-biased, with few elements and generous negative space. Supporting profile-card planes, pearlescent spheres and ribbons recede through diffusion, haze, gentle blur and restrained periwinkle, blush and cyan reflections. It is not hero art and never a dense collage, orbit network or landscape spectacle.
- **Container cube:** one or two opaque UP! container cubes per file, never more than two; twenty cubes across the delivered set. Each is the decision-0013 geometry carried into decision 0014: a solid, closed and sealed monolithic rounded die, smooth continuous top and side planes, exact white “UP!” once on the front, no other readable text. No cube is centred; together they occupy at most 16 percent of the canvas. No hole, opening, slot, lid, rim, cavity, recess, insert, handle, tab or protrusion; nothing enters or emerges; never a flat badge, rounded tile, thin slab or app-icon shape.
- **Quiet composition contract:** decorative objects occupy at most 25 percent of the canvas and at least 65 percent remains visually quiet, recorded as visual review plus the safe-zone proxy rather than claimed automated segmentation. Subjects stay outside the shared pair rectangle with visual separation. A left/right/upper rectangle is at least 56 percent wide and 70 percent high, or a central rectangle covers at least 45 percent of the canvas. The actual pixels must measure light mean luminance at least 220 or dark mean at most 36, with population deviation at most 12. If a proposed zone fails, reduce the rectangle honestly; never loosen the thresholds.
- **Families delivered, light and dark each:** quiet corner (one cube lower right, left 68 percent safe); quiet corner left (one cube lower left, right 68 percent safe); distant horizon (two cubes below a low horizon, upper 72 percent safe); peripheral frame (two cubes at opposite outer corners, central 60 by 76 percent safe); mist orbit right (two cubes in the outer right, left 64 percent safe); mist orbit left (two cubes in the outer left, right 64 percent safe).
- **Measured rectangles (x, y, width, height; light mean/deviation; dark mean/deviation):** quiet corner `0,0,0.68,1`, `245.3/6.2`, `13.4/11.0`; quiet corner left `0.32,0,0.68,1`, `244.4/7.1`, `12.4/10.3`; distant horizon `0,0,1,0.72`, `242.8/10.6`, `17.5/8.6`; peripheral frame `0.2,0.12,0.6,0.76`, `251.9/3.0`, `21.5/9.8`; mist orbit right `0,0,0.64,1`, `241.9/7.7`, `11.6/7.3`; mist orbit left `0.36,0,0.64,1`, `239.9/9.0`, `12.7/5.0`. Every proposed rectangle passed on the actual pixels.
- **References and prompts:** only recorded repository backgrounds were supplied as references—title, slides-v2 or an earlier ambient file—except the unpublished draft in the lineage of `mist-orbit-right-dark.png`, retained by hash only and marked superseded. Public records contain repository-relative paths, never source-machine paths. Every verbatim prompt and generation id is staged in `../assets/generated/backgrounds/slides-v3-ambient/PROMPTS.source.json` and repeated in provenance.
- **Avoid:** people, faces, footwear, rockets, third-party marks, uncontrolled text, watermarks, neon or cyberpunk lighting, transparency, central focal points, high-contrast clutter, dense collage, multiple strong rings or network webs, dramatic mountains, sun or moon. The only readable mark is UP! on the authorized cubes.
- **Formats:** twelve delivered files at the generator's native 1672 by 941, 8-bit opaque RGB with intact C2PA; 1920 by 1080, square and vertical derivatives are not delivered. Light takes ink copy; dark takes white copy. Use the complete file and its measured rectangle; never crop out a cube.
- **Validation:** `node scripts/validate.mjs --only ambient` pins all twelve files, prompt hashes and text, generation ids, public references and superseded hash-only lineage, content-credentials timestamps, cube counts, shared pair rectangles and measurements, overview-sheet pixels, decision language and galleries. Its polarity-aware prompt rules reject affirmative dense or hero requests, more than two cubes, insufficient quiet area, mismatched pair zones, threshold drift, third-party marks, openings, inserts, flat badges and unpinned files. It is separate from `--only branded`; IB-12 retains its existing fixture and tests unchanged.
- **Status and rights:** delivered under `../assets/generated/backgrounds/slides-v3-ambient/`; additive and proposed, not approved brand imagery. The cube is an observed mark shown under decision 0014 for exactly these twelve scenes and the two deterministic overview sheets. The scene pixels follow `../LICENSES/GENERATED-IMAGES.md`; the UP! mark remains its owner's trademark, with no extraction, standalone reuse or trademark licence. A further branded file needs a new recorded owner authorization.

Mascots; neon or cyberpunk key art; generic AI dreamscape landscapes and the parent brand's pink dreamscape covers (the iridescent-horizon family of IB-12 is the one admitted landscape, decision 0013); stock photography of people using phones; any regeneration of the marks.
