# Code of conduct

Everyone who takes part in this project agrees to:

- Be respectful and direct; critique work, not people.
- Keep discussions on evidence: cite the source, the token or the specification.
- Welcome newcomers and answer questions with pointers to the documents.
- Respect the publication boundary: never share private material in issues, pull requests or discussions.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks and publishing others' private information. Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and comments that break these rules and may temporarily or permanently exclude repeat offenders. Concerns are raised privately with the maintainers listed in `GOVERNANCE.md`.
