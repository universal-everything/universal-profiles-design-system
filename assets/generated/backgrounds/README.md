# Slide and background library

Status: proposed (generated for this system under recorded provenance; not approved brand imagery; the expressive and ambient contracts below are separate system rules checked by separate validators; the UP! container cube depicted in slides-v2 and slides-v3-ambient is an observed mark shown under the set-specific owner authorizations in decisions 0012, 0013 and 0014)

Twenty-six 16:9 backgrounds for decks, social titles and documentation: the quiet title pair (IB-10), the six-family expressive `slides-v2` set (IB-12), and the additive six-family `slides-v3-ambient` set (IB-13), with every family in a light and a dark register. Use slides-v2 for campaign, hero, section-divider and closing slides: its layered identity collages are intentionally expressive and dense. Use slides-v3-ambient behind body copy, charts and transparent product screens: it limits each edge-biased scene to one or two cubes and keeps at least 65 percent visually quiet by visual review plus the measured safe-zone proxy. Every file in the two six-family slides-v2 and slides-v3-ambient sets shows one or more official UP! container cubes, solid, closed and sealed; the title pair and the two heroes in `../heroes/` show blank objects only.

Verbatim prompts, generation ids, reference inputs, hashes, dimensions, colour facts, content credentials and measured safe zones are in `../PROVENANCE.json`. The slides-v2 staged prompts and selection stay pinned by `slides-v2/PROMPTS.source.json` and `../../../scripts/validate/branded-backgrounds.json` (`node scripts/validate.mjs --only branded`), including the four existing precise-object edits and their lineage. The ambient prompts and selection are independently pinned by `slides-v3-ambient/PROMPTS.source.json` and `../../../scripts/validate/ambient-backgrounds.json` (`node scripts/validate.mjs --only ambient`); this additive lock does not broaden or weaken slides-v2.

## Files and safe zones

Safe zones are fractions of the width and height, measured on the files (mean luminance and population standard deviation inside the zone) and recorded per file in the provenance record, where the validators re-measure them. Each family shares one rectangle between its light and dark file so a deck keeps its layout when the theme switches. Put copy inside the zone; anything outside it needs the glass tier or a solid tint, and copy never runs over a UP! container cube.

### Ambient zones

The ambient contract is deliberately stricter: a light zone measures at least 220, a dark zone at most 36, and either has deviation at most 12. Each rectangle is at least 56 percent wide and 70 percent high, or is the central zone covering at least 45 percent of the canvas. The six pairs pass on their actual pixels without reducing the proposed rectangles. Together the twelve files contain twenty cubes by visual count, never more than two in one file; the 65 percent visually-quiet requirement is a visual review backed by safe-zone area and luminance/deviation, not a claim of automated object segmentation.

## The container cube

The UP! object in every slides-v2 and slides-v3-ambient file is a solid, closed and sealed container cube: the Universal Profile is a container, an account that holds things, but the cube that stands for it is one whole object. What the profile holds is shown by the scene around the cube, never by opening the cube. The geometry originates in decision 0013 and is carried into the ambient authorization by decision 0014 (`../../../brand/lockups.md`):

- a monolithic rounded die: softly rounded edges, near-equal proportions, glossy periwinkle;
- smooth, continuous top and side planes with no features;
- completely closed and sealed: no hole, slot, lid, rim, cavity or insert, no aperture, recessed shape, receptacle, handle or tab, and no document, card or object entering or emerging from it;
- the exact white UP! mark once, on the front face, and no other readable text;
- never a flat badge, a rounded rectangular tile, a thin slab, a shallow extrusion or a front-on app-icon shape.

The slides-v2 generator received two or three of the repository's already-public onboarding illustrations (`../../slides/onboarding/get-started-2.png`, `get-started-3.png`, `get-started-4.png`) as style and composition references. The ambient generations use only recorded repository backgrounds as geometry or restraint references, plus one hash-only superseded draft in the lineage of `mist-orbit-right-dark.png`; no private path or unpublished file is exposed. The private board exports behind the earlier depictions remain superseded and unpublished.

Every prompt in both `PROMPTS.source.json` files states the closed geometry in the positive and rejects openings or inserts in its Avoid line. The separate branded and ambient validators pin the verbatim prompts and use polarity-aware keyword and pattern gates, so “not hero art” and an Avoid line naming a dense scene remain rejections rather than requests. Those policy checks cannot infer cube geometry or count cubes from pixels: geometry, the twenty ambient cubes and the visually-quiet-area judgement rest on recorded visual review. The live gates do decode pixels to verify headers, safe-zone measurements and deterministic overview composition.

## Light and dark

- **Expressive light register** for ink copy: `text.default` on a slides-v2 safe zone that measures at least 200 with deviation at most 20; headline `type.deck.h1`, body `type.deck.body`, one accent phrase in `accent.brand`.
- **Expressive dark register** for white copy: dark-theme `text.default` on a slides-v2 safe zone that measures at most 64 with deviation at most 20.
- **Ambient light and dark registers** use the stricter IB-13 thresholds: at least 220 for light, at most 36 for dark, deviation at most 12. Use these files when body copy, charts or transparent screens need most of the canvas.
- Pair by family: a deck uses one family per section and switches register with the theme, the way the README hero switches with `prefers-color-scheme`; the safe zone is the same rectangle in both registers.
- Do not tint a light file to make it dark or the reverse; the pair exists for that.
- Secondary text never sits on the busy area: outside the safe zone use the glass panel (`glass-panel` in the components tier) or a solid `surface.card` tint behind the text, per the luminance rule in `../../../foundations/elevation-and-glass.md`.

## Crop and composition

- **Native 16:9** covers a 1920 by 1080 slide with a 15 percent upscale; use "scale to fill", never stretch. For 3840 by 2160 output, regenerate rather than upscale.
- **4:3 (1254 by 941)** and **1:1 (941 by 941)**: keep the side the subject sits on when the image is the point (identity orbits, identity network and iridescent horizon: right; glass profile stack: left; address ribbons and modular constellation: centre) and the opposite side when the copy is the point. A crop keeps the scene around the cubes; never crop down to a cube so that it reads as a mark on its own.
- For ambient files, preserve the recorded safe-zone side and the edge cluster together: quiet corner and mist orbit right keep the right edge; their left companions keep the left edge; distant horizon keeps the full width and low horizon; peripheral frame should remain uncropped when used behind copy.
- **9:16 stories (529 by 941)**: modular constellation crops best (the calm centre column with a cluster above and below); the others lose their subject or their copy space, so use the aura background from `packages/address-signature` instead.
- **Link previews (1200 by 630)**: the title pair or the heroes; the slides-v2 families are too detailed at that size.
- Keep 6 percent margins; page numbers bottom right; the tracked label top left in `type.deck.caption`; the lockup, when used, from official files only and never over the subject. The depicted cubes do not replace the lockup: a slide that needs the mark as a mark uses the official files.
- The showcases in `../../slides/previews/` are worked compositions: three app screens at 299 by 634 on the right half of the title background, the left half free for copy.

## Usage

Deck software: set the file as the slide background (Keynote: Format, Background, Image Fill, Scale to Fill; PowerPoint: Format Background, Picture fill; Google Slides: Background, Choose image), then place text in the safe zone. Choose slides-v2 when the scene carries the section or campaign mood; choose slides-v3-ambient when the content—body copy, a chart or a transparent app screen—must lead.

Web or documentation:

```html
<picture>
  <source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="assets/generated/backgrounds/slides-v3-ambient/mist-orbit-right-dark.png">
  <img alt="" src="assets/generated/backgrounds/slides-v3-ambient/mist-orbit-right-light.png" width="100%">
</picture>
```

```css
.deck-content {
  background: var(--up-surface-canvas) url("assets/generated/backgrounds/slides-v3-ambient/mist-orbit-right-light.png") center / cover no-repeat;
  color: var(--up-text-default);
  padding: 6%;
  max-width: 64%; /* the ambient family's measured safe-zone width */
}
@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark) {
  .deck-content { background-image: url("assets/generated/backgrounds/slides-v3-ambient/mist-orbit-right-dark.png"); }
}
```

Alt text: describe the picture when it carries meaning (the gallery table above has one line per file, naming the UP! container cubes); an empty `alt` when it is decoration behind copy.

## Publication boundary

These files are public and may be reused under the repository licence as whole backgrounds. They are backgrounds, not marks: the UP! mark remains a trademark of its owner and is not licensed for extraction or standalone reuse. Do not crop a cube out, trace it, vectorise it, use it as a logo, icon, sticker, lockup or app icon, alter it, or present it as an official mark file; do not edit a background into a logo, place it behind a mark reconstruction or present it as a product screenshot. Decisions 0012 and 0013 cover exactly the twelve expressive scenes and their two overview sheets; decision 0014 covers exactly the twelve ambient scenes and their two overview sheets and grants no extraction or trademark licence. The public references are repository-relative and the one unpublished ambient draft is retained by hash-only superseded lineage; no local path, mark master, private render or export enters the repository (`../../../decisions/0010-no-reconstruction-under-any-label.md`, OPEN-03). New members must be generated, carry a provenance entry with the prompt and hash, receive a new recorded owner authorization when they show the mark, and pass the appropriate IB-12 or IB-13 contract.

## Adding variations

1. Choose the expressive IB-12 frame or the quiet IB-13 ambient frame; do not blend the two contracts. Reuse the matching set's prompt source while keeping the closed-cube wording, object-count limit, safe-zone composition and Avoid line, then generate the light and dark companion from the same family description. Any variation that shows the UP! mark needs its own recorded owner authorization and decision before it enters the repository: decisions 0012/0013 cover only the twelve slides-v2 scenes and their two overview sheets, and decision 0014 only the twelve slides-v3-ambient scenes and their two overview sheets.
2. Keep the native output; do not upscale, crop or strip the manifest.
3. Name the pair `<family>-light.png` and `<family>-dark.png`, put it in the matching set directory, and add its verbatim prompts, generation ids and public relative references to that set's `PROMPTS.source.json`.
4. Run `node scripts/inspect-png.mjs <file> --grid`, then `--zone x,y,w,h` on both registers until one shared rectangle passes the matching contract. Add both entries to `../PROVENANCE.json` with the measured figures, cube count, prompt, brief, generation inputs and embedded-mark boundary; raise only that set's count and family list.
5. Recompose the matching contact sheets with `node scripts/compose-previews.mjs` and record the placements and hashes in `../../slides/previews/PROVENANCE.json`. Extend only the matching fixture: `branded-backgrounds.json` and `--only branded` for IB-12, or `ambient-backgrounds.json` and `--only ambient` for IB-13. Run `--only assets`, `--only rasters`, both background locks and `npm test` without weakening slides-v2.
